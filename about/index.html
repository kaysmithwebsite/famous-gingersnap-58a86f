About

Kaylin Smith

Real Estate Broker | Investor | Strategic Advisor

Real estate decisions deserve more than a transaction. They deserve a strategy.

Who I am

Real estate decisions deserve more than a transaction.

I help first-time homebuyers, investors, homeowners and sellers across Markham, Toronto, Durham Region and the GTA make informed real estate decisions with confidence.

I am a Real Estate Broker with RE/MAX Epic Realty Inc., Brokerage, and an investor with more than a decade of experience in real estate and investing.

My perspective on real estate comes from both sides of the transaction. I am a real estate professional and an investor myself. My first investment property was a condominium in Richmond Hill, purchased in 2006.

Over the years I have developed a broader understanding of the financial, business and strategic considerations that can influence a real estate decision. My background in financial services, business and consulting allows me to look beyond the immediate transaction and help clients understand the numbers, the opportunity, the risks and the bigger picture.

Buying a first home, building an investment portfolio, preparing to sell, working out whether to move at all: the approach is the same.

  • Understand the goal.
  • Know the market.
  • Build the strategy.
  • Execute with confidence.

Real estate is one of the biggest financial decisions many people make. You deserve advice that is thoughtful, informed and aligned with where you want to go.

Who I help

Real estate with a strategy behind it.

I work with clients across Markham, Toronto, Durham Region and the GTA, with a particular focus on first-time homebuyers and real estate investors.

01

First-time homebuyers

Your first home is more than a purchase. It can be the foundation of your financial future.

I help first-time buyers understand the process, evaluate properties, understand the market and make decisions with confidence.

The goal is not simply to find a home. It is to make the right move for your circumstances and your future.

02

Investors

Real estate investing requires more than finding a property that looks good.

I help investors evaluate opportunities, understand the numbers, consider market conditions and think strategically about how a property fits into their broader goals.

My own experience as a real estate investor allows me to approach these conversations from an investor's perspective as well as a real estate professional's.

03

Sellers

Selling is about more than putting a property on the market.

I help sellers develop a strategy around pricing, positioning, marketing, negotiation and market conditions to give their property the strongest possible presentation and representation.

Every property and seller is different, so the strategy should be too.

04

Pre-construction

Pre-construction can involve significant financial commitments, timelines and long-term considerations.

I help clients evaluate opportunities, understand the financial considerations and determine whether a project makes sense for their goals before they commit.

My approach

Strategy before the transaction.

Great real estate representation starts with understanding the person, not just the property. Before recommending a property or strategy, I want to understand:

  • What are you trying to accomplish?
  • What does the market mean for your situation?
  • What do the numbers actually tell us?
  • What are the risks?
  • What opportunities should we consider?
  • What makes sense for your short and long-term goals?

I combine market knowledge, research, investment perspective, financial awareness, negotiation and honest advice to help clients make informed decisions.

I do not believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. I believe in asking the right questions, doing the work behind the scenes and giving clients the information they need to make decisions they feel confident about.

No pressure. No unnecessary hype. No telling you what you want to hear just to get the deal.

Clear advice. Strong representation. A strategy built around you.

Experience

A broader perspective on real estate.

My real estate career is supported by experience in investing, financial services, business development and strategic consulting. That background has shaped the way I approach real estate.

I understand that a real estate decision can affect much more than a transaction. It can impact your finances, investment strategy, business, family and long-term plans.

That experience gives me a broader perspective when working with clients, particularly entrepreneurs, investors and clients whose real estate decisions are connected to larger financial or business goals.

My job is to help you understand the opportunity, navigate the process and make a move that makes sense for you.

Giving Back / Community

Giving back matters.

Success is about more than what you build for yourself. It is also about what you contribute to the people and communities around you.

I am proud to support organizations and initiatives that create meaningful opportunities for children, families and communities.

Children's Miracle Network↗ (opens in a new tab)

I have been a proud supporter of Children's Miracle Network for more than a decade.

Through my real estate business, a portion of the proceeds from the sale of every home I sell is donated to Children's Miracle Network, allowing my business and my clients' transactions to contribute to supporting children and families.

Durham Catholic Children's Foundation↗ (opens in a new tab)

I serve as a Community Representative on the Board of Directors of the Durham Catholic Children's Foundation (DCCF), supporting its work to provide resources, opportunities, and support to children and families across Durham Region.

Plan International Canada↗ (opens in a new tab)

I have proudly sponsored children through Plan International Canada for more than two decades.

My long-standing commitment reflects my belief in creating opportunities and supporting children and communities around the world.

Embers for Access Foundation↗ (opens in a new tab)

I support Embers for Access Foundation through sponsorship, community partnerships, fundraising and event initiatives focused on accessibility, inclusion and creating opportunities for individuals and families.

Broader community involvement

My community involvement also includes serving as a director, consultant, sponsor, event leader and community partner across charitable, arts, culture and community initiatives.
