Kay Smith Consulting Group

Strategy for the business, and the room.

A separate practice from the real estate brokerage. Consulting for owner-led businesses on positioning, growth, partnerships and the events that carry them.

Book a Consulting Call →

Owner-led businesses rarely fail on effort. They stall on positioning, on operations that were never designed, and on decisions the owner is too close to make.

Engagements are scoped to a question and produce something written. Where an event is involved, the person who set the objective stays through delivery, so the brief and the execution do not drift apart.

Separate from real estate

Consulting Group work is a distinct practice with its own clients and its own intake. It is not a route into a property transaction, and a real estate enquiry will not be answered from here.

Services

Seven ways this practice is used.

01

Strategic Business Consulting

Who the business is for, what it competes against in a buyer's mind, and what it should stop doing. Most pricing problems turn out to be positioning problems.

02

Business Development

Where the next customers actually come from, and building a way to reach them that does not depend on the owner's calendar.

03

Growth Strategy

What to scale and what to leave alone. Turning what lives in the owner's head into a process someone else can run.

04

Brand Partnerships & Activations

Finding partners whose audience overlaps yours, building an offer worth saying yes to, and running the activation properly.

05

Event Strategy

What the event is for, what it will cost, and whether the format can carry the gap between the two. Concept, budget, vendors, run of show and the debrief most events skip.

06

Speaking

Panels, workshops and keynotes on real estate, business building and brand. Available for corporate, community and industry events.

07

Mentorship

One to one work with agents and early-stage owners on positioning, pipeline and the habits that compound. Limited by design.
