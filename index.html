Meet Kaylin

Representation built around the decision, not the transaction.

Kaylin Smith is a Broker with RE/MAX Epic Realty Inc., Brokerage, working with buyers, sellers and investors across Markham, Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area.

Most of the consequential decisions in a move are made before anyone books a showing. Budget. Location. Timeline. Whether to buy first or sell first. Those decisions constrain everything after them, and they are where the real work belongs.
